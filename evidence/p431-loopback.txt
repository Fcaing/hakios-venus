# p431 - MHI data plane, LOOPBACK ch0 -> ch1

Source: `venus-recon/p431.txt`, extracted from this round's loader log
(`fastboot oem dump logdump` -> `cur-p431.bin` -> ASCII filter).
Round tag in the log is `pcie_tag=p431`; the probe that printed these lines is `apply-390`.

What it shows: the host posted a 16-byte payload on ch0 (`tre ptr 0xa03d0000 len 0x10`) and
two receive buffers on ch1, then rang the channel-0 doorbell. At t=0 the buffers held:

    buffer a: 534f494b 534f494c 534f494d 534f494e    (little-endian: 4b 49 4f 53 4c 49 4f 53 ...)
    buffer b: beef0000 beef0001 beef0002 beef0003    (its own prefill, untouched)

Buffer a is the payload the host wrote to ch0, byte for byte. er1 then held two events whose
`dword3` low byte names the channel: `...00220000` (type 0x22, ch0) and `...01220000`
(type 0x22, ch1).

Conclusion: on a channel pair the firmware does provide (LOOPBACK), host TRE consumption,
payload DMA and event delivery are all correct. What is missing on ch20/ch21 is therefore
not the host's mechanics.
